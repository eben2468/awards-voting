## Getting Started

### Account Registration and Setup

#### Initial Signup Process
1. **Navigate to Registration Page**
   - Go to the website's homepage
   - Click on "Register" or "Sign Up" button
   - Select "Organizer" as your account type

2. **Complete Registration Form**
   - Provide your full name
   - Enter a valid email address (this will be your login)
   - Create a strong password (minimum 8 characters)
   - Confirm your password
   - Agree to terms and conditions

3. **Email Verification**
   - Check your email inbox for verification message
   - Click the verification link to activate your account
   - You'll be redirected to login page upon successful verification

4. **First Login**
   - Navigate to `/organizer/` or click "Organizer Portal" login option
   - Enter your email and password
   - You'll be directed to your organizer dashboard

#### Initial Setup Checklist
After logging in for the first time, complete these essential setup steps:

1. **Complete Your Profile**
   - Visit "My Profile" section
   - Upload a professional profile photo
   - Add your phone number for communication
   - Update any missing information

2. **Configure Notification Preferences**
   - Set up email notifications for important events
   - Enable alerts for new votes and system updates

3. **Review Platform Guidelines**
   - Read through terms of service
   - Understand platform fees and payout structure
   - Familiarize yourself with content policies

## Managing Events

### Creating a New Event

#### Step 1: Access Event Creation
1. From dashboard, click "New Event" button
2. Or navigate to "Events" → "Add Event"

#### Step 2: Event Details
Fill in the following required information:

**Basic Information:**
- **Event Title**: Clear, descriptive name for your awards
- **Description**: Detailed explanation of the event
- **Start Date**: When voting opens
- **End Date**: When voting closes
- **Status**: Draft (for preparation) or Active (immediately live)

**Visual Elements:**
- **Banner Image**: Upload eye-catching promotional image (recommended dimensions: 1200x600px)
- **Logo**: Your organization/event logo

**Settings:**
- **Voting Method**: SMS, USSD, Web, or combination
- **Payment Requirements**: Free voting or paid per vote
- **Multiple Votes**: Allow users to vote multiple times
- **Verification**: Email/SMS verification requirements

#### Step 3: Review and Publish
1. Preview your event details
2. Verify all dates and settings
3. Save as "Draft" for later editing or "Publish" to go live immediately

### Editing Existing Events

#### Access Event Editor
1. Navigate to "Events" section
2. Find your event in the list
3. Click the pencil icon (Edit) next to the event

#### Editable Fields
- **Title and Description**: Update event details
- **Dates**: Modify start/end dates (be cautious with active events)
- **Status**: Change between draft, active, or completed
- **Banner/Logo**: Upload new images
- **Settings**: Adjust voting rules and payment options

#### Important Notes
- Cannot edit dates of active events that have received votes
- Changing payment settings affects future votes only
- Status changes may impact user accessibility

### Event Management Best Practices

#### Before Launch
- Test voting process with sample nominees
- Verify all dates and times are correct
- Ensure banner images are properly formatted
- Review and proofread all text content

#### During Event
- Monitor vote counts regularly
- Respond promptly to user inquiries
- Watch for any technical issues
- Track engagement metrics

#### After Completion
- Download final reports
- Process winner announcements
- Handle payout requests
- Archive event data

## Managing Categories

### Creating Award Categories

#### Access Category Management
1. Navigate to "Categories" in sidebar
2. Click "Add Category" button

#### Category Setup
**Required Information:**
- **Name**: Clear category title (e.g., "Best Actor", "Song of the Year")
- **Event**: Select which event this category belongs to
- **Description**: Brief explanation of the award criteria
- **Display Order**: Position in which categories appear (lower numbers first)

**Settings:**
- **Status**: Active or Inactive
- **Multiple Winners**: Allow ties or single winner
- **Judging Criteria**: Optional detailed judging information

### Organizing Categories Effectively

#### Best Practices
- Create 5-15 categories per event for optimal engagement
- Use clear, recognizable category names
- Group similar awards together logically
- Consider having main categories and sub-categories
- Maintain consistent naming conventions

#### Category Management Tips
- Arrange categories by importance or popularity
- Deactivate unused categories rather than deleting them
- Regularly review category performance
- Update descriptions based on voter feedback

### Editing Categories

#### Access Category Editor
1. Go to "Categories" section
2. Locate the category you want to edit
3. Click the pencil icon to modify

#### Editable Elements
- **Name and Description**: Update category details
- **Event Association**: Move category between events (if no votes exist)
- **Display Order**: Reorganize category positioning
- **Status**: Activate or deactivate categories

## Processing Payouts

### Understanding Revenue Structure

#### Earnings Calculation
- **Per-Vote Revenue**: Fixed amount earned per legitimate vote
- **Platform Fees**: Percentage deducted for system maintenance
- **Payment Processing**: Fees for transaction handling
- **Net Earnings**: Your actual take-home amount

#### Payment Timeline
- **Processing Period**: 7-14 days after event completion
- **Verification Time**: System validates all transactions
- **Payout Request**: Submit withdrawal request through portal
- **Transfer Time**: 3-5 business days for funds to arrive

### Setting Up Payment Methods

#### Adding Bank Information
1. Navigate to "Payouts" section
2. Click "Add Method" button
3. Select payment method type:
   - **Bank Transfer**: Direct deposit to bank account
   - **Mobile Money**: MTN Mobile Money, Vodafone Cash, etc.
   - **PayPal**: International payment option

#### Required Information
- **Method Type**: Select from available options
- **Method Name**: Descriptive label for this payment method
- **Account Name**: Full name on the account
- **Account Number**: Valid banking/mobile money number
- **Bank Branch**: Physical branch location (for bank transfers)

### Requesting Payouts

#### Payout Process Steps
1. **Check Available Balance**: Verify funds ready for withdrawal
2. **Select Event**: Choose which event's earnings to withdraw
3. **Choose Payment Method**: Select previously configured method
4. **Enter Amount**: Specify withdrawal amount (subject to minimum)
5. **Submit Request**: Send payout request for processing

#### Payout Status Tracking
- **Pending**: Request submitted, awaiting approval
- **Processing**: Funds being transferred
- **Completed**: Successfully deposited
- **Rejected**: Issue with request (insufficient funds, invalid method)

### Financial Reporting

#### Transaction History
- **Earnings Records**: Detailed breakdown of income sources
- **Withdrawal History**: All payout requests and their status
- **Fee Deductions**: Transparent listing of all charges
- **Balance Tracking**: Running total of available funds

#### Tax Considerations
- **Income Documentation**: Generate reports for tax purposes
- **Transaction Records**: Maintain detailed financial logs
- **Professional Advice**: Consult tax professionals for obligations

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### Login Problems
**Issue**: Cannot access organizer portal
**Solutions**:
- Verify correct email and password
- Check email for account verification
- Reset password using "Forgot Password" link
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try different browser or device

#### Event Creation Errors
**Issue**: Unable to create or save events
**Solutions**:
- Check all required fields are completed
- Verify image file formats and sizes
- Ensure dates are logical (start before end)
- Confirm you haven't exceeded event limits
- Contact support with error details

#### Voting System Issues
**Issue**: Votes not recording properly
**Solutions**:
- Test voting process with sample accounts
- Check event status is set to "Active"
- Verify payment settings are correct
- Clear browser data and try again
- Report persistent issues to technical team

#### Payment Processing Problems
**Issue**: Payout requests failing or delayed
**Solutions**:
- Verify bank/account information is correct
- Check minimum withdrawal amounts
- Confirm sufficient available balance
- Review payout processing schedules
- Contact financial support team

### When to Contact Support

#### Technical Support Needed For:
- System errors preventing normal operations
- Security concerns or suspected breaches
- Payment processing failures
- Data loss or corruption issues
- Integration problems with external systems

#### Customer Service For:
- Account access and verification issues
- Billing and payment inquiries
- Policy and guideline questions
- Feature requests and suggestions
- Training and educational needs

### Emergency Procedures

#### Critical Issue Response
1. **Immediate Assessment**: Determine severity and impact
2. **Documentation**: Record all relevant details and timestamps
3. **Containment**: Prevent issue from spreading or worsening
4. **Communication**: Inform affected parties promptly
5. **Resolution**: Work with support team for fix
6. **Follow-up**: Verify complete resolution and prevent recurrence

#### Backup Plans
- **Alternative Communication**: Secondary contact methods
- **Manual Processes**: Paper-based procedures when digital fails
- **External Resources**: Partner services for critical functions
- **Contingency Budgets**: Reserved funds for emergency situations
